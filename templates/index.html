Discover Your Hidden Skills ✦

Answer these six questions honestly. There are no right or wrong answers. Your responses help identify patterns in your interests and working style.

1. What do you enjoy doing when you face a difficult problem?

Choose what sounds most like you.

2. Which activity sounds most interesting to you?

Think about what you would enjoy most.

3. How do you prefer to work?

Choose your comfortable working style.

4. Which area interests you the most?

Choose a subject you would like to explore.

5. How do you learn something new?

Choose what helps you understand a new topic.

6. Which task would you choose for a college project?

Choose the task you would enjoy most.

Upload Your Resume

Upload your resume in PDF or TXT format. Choose a target career to receive more relevant skill recommendations.

Supported formats: PDF and TXT. Maximum file size: 5 MB.

Your resume is processed by the backend for analysis and is not permanently saved by this feature. The score is an indicative, rule-based assessment, not a professional ATS score or a hiring decision. Only add suggested skills if they genuinely reflect your abilities.
